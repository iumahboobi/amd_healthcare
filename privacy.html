Privacy notice

We built this network on trust. Below is a plain-English explanation of what data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and how to reach us if you want to correct or delete anything.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller for this website is the coordinating group behind Afghan Medical Diaspora, currently operating from Germany. In the context of this founding-phase project, data is handled privately by the small coordinating team and is not transferred to any third-party analytics, advertising, or mailing platform.

2. What data we collect and when

We only collect data that you voluntarily give us through two forms on the site:

Join the Network form

  • Name, email address, phone number (optional), country of residence, preferred language
  • Profession, primary specialty, licence issuing country (optional), years of experience (optional)
  • Preferred contribution ways, availability, preferred Afghan location
  • Additional message (free text)

Contact form

  • Name, email address, subject, and your message

3. What we do NOT ask for at this stage

On purpose, the registration form does not ask you to upload passport scans, medical licence files, certificates, CVs, or identity documents. A later, separate and clearly-communicated credential-verification step (only for members who actually want to participate in a concrete pilot) may request a subset of those documents directly and securely — never through this public website form.

4. Why we collect it (purpose and legal basis)

Data is processed solely for the purpose of building the Afghan Medical Diaspora network and coordinating with registered members about potential future pilot projects, training, mentorship, and partnership opportunities. We do not sell data, we do not share it with recruiters, and we do not use it for unsolicited marketing unrelated to this initiative.

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the legal basis for processing your data submitted via the Join the Network or Contact form is your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You give this consent by ticking the required checkboxes before submitting any form, and you can withdraw it at any time (see section 8 below).

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics script. A visitor's browser may store technical data required to render the page (standard HTTP logs, cache) but no identifier is intentionally created or persisted by us across visits.

6. Retention period

We keep the data you submit for as long as the Afghan Medical Diaspora initiative is actively coordinated, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first. If the initiative is formally wound down, we will securely destroy all personal data within three months and notify any registered members whose contact details we still hold.

7. Third-party sharing

We do not share, rent, or sell your data to any third party. If and only if you later consent to participate in a concrete pilot, the minimum information required for that pilot (e.g. your name, specialty, and direct contact) may be shared with a named partner healthcare facility — we will tell you who the partner is, and ask for your explicit permission, before any such disclosure.

8. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you (right of access)
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data (right to rectification)
  • Ask us to delete your data entirely (right to erasure / "right to be forgotten")
  • Ask us to restrict processing while a request is being reviewed
  • Withdraw consent at any time (withdrawing consent does not affect lawfulness of processing done before withdrawal)
  • Lodge a complaint with a German data protection supervisory authority if you believe our handling is non-compliant

9. How to exercise your rights

For any data request — access, correction, deletion, or consent withdrawal — email us directly at privacy@afghanmedicaldiaspora.org. Requests are typically answered within 14 calendar days. We may ask for a simple confirmation of identity (e.g. reply from the email address on file) before disclosing or altering data, to protect your privacy.

10. International transfers

At this founding stage, data is stored and processed exclusively on European / German infrastructure managed by the small coordinating team. No personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

11. Security

We take reasonable organisational and technical precautions to protect submissions against unauthorised access, including access restricted to the small coordinating team, password-protected storage, and no public cloud spreadsheet of member data. That said, this is a volunteer-run project in its founding phase, so we cannot promise bank-grade infrastructure — if your risk tolerance requires that level, you may prefer to wait until a more structured operating entity is in place before registering.

12. Children

Our forms and network are not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted data to us, please contact the privacy email above and we will remove it promptly.

13. Changes to this notice

If we materially change how we collect, use, or share data, we will update this page, bump the "last updated" date below, and — if we have a way to contact you — notify registered members via email.
